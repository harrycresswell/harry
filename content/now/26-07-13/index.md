![A view of the Mike O'Callaghan–Pat Tillman Memorial Bridge spanning the Colorado River below, on a bright but cloudy day, as seen from the Hoover Dam.](https://res.cloudinary.com/harrycresswell/image/upload/v1785755962/hc/harry-cresswell-hoover-dam.jpg)

I’m standing in the middle of the [Hoover Dam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoover_Dam), looking out across at the [Mike O'Callaghan–Pat Tillman Memorial Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_O%27Callaghan%E2%80%93Pat_Tillman_Memorial_Bridge) which sits opposite the dam from and spans the Colorado River below. To my left is Arizona, my right Nevada. 

It’s impossible to appreciate the size of this thing until you’re actually here standing on top of it. 

Apparently the dam’s hydroelectric power plant produces around 4 billion kilowatt-hours of electricity annually and powers the whole of Las Vegas, along with other areas of Nevada, Arizona and California.
