I’m in Munich for a long weekend, here visiting a friend I met in Cape Town in 2022.

Right now we’re down at the [Eisbachwelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eisbach_(Isar)), watching surfers brave the cold water at this iconic river surfing spot. Apparently people have been surfing this accidental rapid wave since the early 1970’s. 

A land-locked city, closer to the alps than the ocean, with a vibrant surf scene... who would have thought!
