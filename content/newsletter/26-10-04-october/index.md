All my focus has been on client commitments these past four weeks, so personal projects have taken a backseat sadly. It’s meant I’ve had to push the launch of [Practical Hugo](https://practicalhugo.com/) season 2 to December/early January. 

I know this won’t come as good news to those of you I’ve spoken to who are keen to continue the course. But unfortunately something had to give as I’m taking a break in November to go travelling. I’ll do my best to update [my now page](/now/) whilst I’m away for anyone interested in my whereabouts!

As per usual, here are my highlights from the past month, a few good links from around the web and what I have planned for the month ahead.


## Highlights from last month

Enjoyed an overnight stay at [Farnham Castle](https://www.farnhamcastle.com/) in Surrey where we were celebrating the wedding of two close friends.

Ran [a new PB at the Shoreditch Half](/now/26-09-20/). I have the Runna app and [SAP](https://sapgoodenergy.com/) to thank for this, which made the training quite a bit more fun than usual and race day that bit easier.

Celebrated my godson’s 1st birthday with a picnic in the park.

## Elsewhere on the web

[Building an independent design practice](https://www.gabrielvaldivia.com/notes/building-an-independent-design-practice) from Gabriel Valdivia.

Gabriel reflects on how 10,000 hours working independently has shaped his design practice and what he’s learnt along the way. Lot’s of great advise to take from this one.

[Using light-dark() without getting caught out ](https://www.tempertemper.net/blog/using-light-dark-without-getting-caught-out) from Martin Underhill.

Martin clearly explains how to create light and dark mode correctly with the `light-dark()` CSS function, how to deal with fallbacks for browsers that don’t support it, and the difference it makes from the more commonly used media query approach.

[Dark Forestry](https://henry.codes/writing/dark-forestry/) from Henry Desroches.

> “There has never been a better time for us to develop a system of principles and ethics...there has never been a better time to develop a strong sense of empathy for our neighbors...take a stance of harm reduction...
>
> Slow down and [make your own days](https://www.ronnowpoetry.com/contents/ohara/TrueAccount.html#:~:text=And%20now,eyes%2E%22), rather than let them be made for you. We must be intentional, active members of our communities if we are to understand how we can serve them better.“

[Towards a personal online third-space](https://ohhelloana.blog/mkdn/) from Ana Rodrigues.

I enjoyed reading about Ana’s IndieWeb journey and in the process discovered Paul’s brilliant [beginner’s guide to the IndieWeb](https://paulrobertlloyd.com/2024/201/s1/peckham_digital/), based on a presentation from a good while back.
